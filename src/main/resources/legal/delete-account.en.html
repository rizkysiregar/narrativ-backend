Account Deletion

Last updated: November 14, 2026

You can delete your NARRATIV account and your personal data at any time.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the NARRATIV app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab and tap the Settings icon.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Enter your password, or choose Verify with Google if you signed in with Google, then confirm.

If you cannot open the app

Email {{contactEmail}} from the email address registered to your account, with the subject "Delete account". We will verify your request before deleting the account.

What is deleted

  • Your email, username, name, phone number, password, and Google account link are replaced so they can no longer be tied to you.
  • Your profile picture is deleted.
  • All of your posts, with their photos and videos, are deleted.
  • The text of your comments is blanked.
  • Your likes, followers, followed accounts, and block list are removed.
  • All sign-in sessions and devices registered for notifications are removed.

What may be kept

  • Moderation report records and audit records needed for security and rule enforcement. These no longer contain your identifiable personal data.

Important

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Afterwards you can sign up again with the same email as a new account. See also the Privacy Policy.
