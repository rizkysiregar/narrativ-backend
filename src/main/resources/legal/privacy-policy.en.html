Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

NARRATIV is an app for sharing posts made of text, photos, and videos, and for interacting through comments and followers. This policy explains what data we collect, what we use it for, who it is shared with, and how you can control it.

1. Data we collect

Data you provide

  • Account: username, email address, password (stored as a hash, never as plain text), first and last name, and a phone number if you provide one.
  • Sign in with Google: if you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID, email address, name, and Google profile picture.
  • Profile: the profile picture you upload.
  • Content: your posts (text, photos, videos), comments, likes, and the accounts you follow or block.
  • Reports: reports you submit about other posts, comments, or accounts.

Data collected automatically

  • Device: push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging), device ID, platform, app version, operating system version, device model, language, and time zone. We use it so notifications reach the right device.
  • Technical: IP address and server request logs, used for security and to limit abuse.

We do not broadly access your contacts, location, or gallery. Photos and videos are only taken from files you pick yourself.

2. How we use data

  • To create and manage your account and verify your identity when you sign in.
  • To show posts, comments, profiles, and follower relationships.
  • To send notifications, for example when someone replies to your comment, and emails with one-time codes (OTP) to reset your password.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent spam and abuse, and act on user reports.
  • To fix and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not show ads based on your profile.

3. Who we share data with

  • Other users: your username, profile picture, posts, and comments can be seen by other users. Your email address and phone number are not shown to other users.
  • Google: for Sign in with Google and for push notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Email delivery provider: to send one-time codes and notification emails.
  • Authorities: when required by applicable law.

4. How long we keep data

  • Account data and content are kept while your account is active.
  • Sign-in sessions (refresh tokens) last at most 30 days and are removed when you log out.
  • When you delete your account, your personal data is anonymized immediately, your posts and their photos are deleted, and your comments are blanked. Details are on the Account Deletion page.
  • Moderation reports and audit records may be kept longer where needed for security and rule enforcement.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. You can edit your profile and delete posts or your account directly in the app (Settings > Delete account). For any other request, contact us at {{contactEmail}}.

6. Security

All communication between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored as hashes. Access to data is restricted. No system is completely risk free, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

NARRATIV is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children below that age. If you believe this has happened, contact us so we can delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date of the latest update is shown above, and for important changes we will let you know in the app.

9. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to {{contactEmail}}.
